Hiragana is one of the two Japanese syllabic alphabets, used to write native Japanese words and grammatical elements. There are 46 base characters, each representing a distinct syllable, plus 25 dakuten (voiced) and handakuten variants.

This chart covers all hiragana columns — vowels (a i u e o), then ka, sa, ta, na, ha, ma, ya, ra, wa rows, plus the voiced dakuten variants. Select any column to add it to your study queue, then practice in Study mode with score and time tracking.
